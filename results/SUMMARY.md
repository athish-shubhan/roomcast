# RoomCast results

One 2-vCPU VM. The edge is pinned to CPU 0 with GOMAXPROCS=1; the publisher and the viewer fleet share CPU 1. Latency is publish-to-client from the publisher's timestamp (same host clock), measured by the viewer fleet. Generated by `bench/plot.py` from `results/*.jsonl`.

## 1. Memory per idle connection

Viewers connect, join a room and stay idle (publisher sends nothing). Edge process numbers are deltas from the empty edge after a forced GC; host MemAvailable drop also counts kernel socket memory for both ends of every connection (client and server on this box).

| transport | connections | goroutines | edge RSS per conn | Go heap per conn | goroutine stacks per conn | host memory per conn (both socket ends) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| std | 5,000 | 10,009 | 6.5 KiB | 1.8 KiB | 4.0 KiB | 17.2 KiB |
| std | 10,000 | 20,009 | 6.4 KiB | 1.8 KiB | 4.0 KiB | 16.0 KiB |
| std | 18,000 | 36,009 | 6.4 KiB | 1.8 KiB | 4.0 KiB | 14.7 KiB |
| epoll | 5,000 | 9 | 0.6 KiB | 0.4 KiB | 0.0 KiB | 19.3 KiB |
| epoll | 10,000 | 9 | 0.5 KiB | 0.4 KiB | 0.0 KiB | 8.8 KiB |
| epoll | 18,000 | 9 | 0.5 KiB | 0.4 KiB | 0.0 KiB | 9.8 KiB |

At 18,000 connections the event-loop edge uses 12.0x less process memory per idle connection (0.5 vs 6.4 KiB): the goroutine-per-connection build keeps two parked goroutines per connection (36,009 goroutines, 4.0 KiB of stacks per connection) and their per-connection state. Everything else in the host column (about 8 to 9 KiB per connection) is the kernel's socket memory for both ends plus the viewer fleet's own state, which does not depend on the edge build. This VM's 20,000 file-descriptor limit per process capped the test at 18,000 connections.

## 3. Slow viewers

2% of viewers read at 8 KB/s through a 4 KiB receive buffer. A viewer whose socket backs up gets only moderation, auction, battle and gift frames until it catches up (its comments are dropped), its queue keeps only the latest auction and battle values, and edge send buffers are kept at 16 KiB so the backlog stays where it can be coalesced. Everyone else should not notice.

| run | normal viewers: auction p99 ms (1x) | slow viewers: auction/battle p50 / p99 ms | comments dropped | kicked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| slow-std | 112.2 | 112 / 251 | 5,671,168 | 0 |
| slow-epoll | 125.9 | 112 / 316 | 4,723,420 | 0 |

## 4. Killing an edge with 8,000 viewers

Two edges, 8,000 viewers each. Edge 1 gets SIGKILL 25 s in; its viewers reconnect to edge 2, which admits at most 5,000 new connections per second (burst 500) and answers the rest with a retry-after hint. naive: retry every 50 ms and ignore hints. jitter: honour the hint plus exponential backoff with jitter.

| policy | lost | recovered | p50 s | p99 s | max s | retry responses | auction p99 ms in the 10 s after the kill | auction wrong at end | moderation behind at end |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| naive | 8000 | 8000 | 0.97 | 1.72 | 1.72 | 17,825 | 100 | 0 of 2455 | 0 of 2455 |
| jitter | 8000 | 8000 | 1.07 | 2.44 | 3.24 | 4,560 | 141 | 0 of 2455 | 0 of 2455 |
